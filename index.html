03 / A book-length inquiry

The End of Knowing

In development

Artificial intelligence is not merely changing how we find answers. It is forcing us to reconsider how we recognize, cultivate, and trust knowledge itself.

This nonfiction book examines the changing relationship among human expertise, judgment, education, institutions, and intelligent machines. Its scope extends beyond classrooms to the wider social and historical consequences of delegating acts of knowing.

The cover is a temporary design concept. No publication date or publisher is claimed.
